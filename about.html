About

Photo: Univ.-Prof. Dr. João Alves

Ernst Koslitsch is a Vienna-based sculptor working under the framework of Yellow Universe, an ongoing body of work built from yellow construction-grade timber, hand cut and stacked. The practice includes monumental Watcher figures, swarm-wall installations of smaller carved forms, and site-specific interventions. His most recent solo exhibition, Cosmic Trigger – Respawn, was shown at Bildraum 07, Vienna, in spring 2026.
