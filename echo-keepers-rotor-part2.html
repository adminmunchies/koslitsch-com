A Parable for Endings and Beginnings We Don't Know Yet, Part 2: The Colossal Land Animal

Echo Keepers 1 + 2, 2026. Installation view, A Parable for Endings and Beginnings, rotor, Graz. Photo: Thomas Raggam

A Parable for Endings and Beginnings We Don't Know Yet continues at rotor with its second chapter, The Colossal Land Animal, one of several mythological creatures explored across the series. Ernst Koslitsch's Echo Keepers 1 + 2, two sculptures over three metres tall built from used formwork panels, remain installed at the entrance to rotor throughout the exhibition.
